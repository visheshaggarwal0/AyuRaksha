Non-obviousness

‘The examiner rejects the application based in §102/103 in combination with
MPEP § 2113.

Essential forthe present invention isthe incubation step, where seeds mixed with
water are incubated for a certain time so that the sprouting initiates. Typically, the
incubation step takes 24 hours, see eg. examples 1 and 14 of the description. In the
incubation stp the seeds have swelled and the weight increased but a sprout is usually not
visible when the heat treetment is commenced. As itis well known from brewing, the
sprouting process generates chemical substances which are not otherwise present or not
‘present in the same amount. While itis not wished to be bound by a certain theory itis
presently believed that the compounds generated during the sprouting process eventually
appear in the extract. In the prior art processes not including an incubation step as
disclosed inthe present application the special compounds produced during sprouting is
not present at al or not present insufficient amounts inthe final extract.
