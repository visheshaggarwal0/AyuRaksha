The Examining Division cannot follow Applicant's arguments.

Cited document D3 discloses wheat starch having an amylose content of 47-55% as
well as elevated resistant starch levels (see abstract). Food products comprising said
starch could only be distinguished from those as presently claimed by the present
DNA comprising the respective mutations. However depending on the food, this
difference may not be detectable any more. After processing of the food which may
comprise procedures like ultra-heating and acid treatment, the DNA may no longer be
detectable any more in the end product. In consequence, the claims are considered to
comprise food products which cannot be distinguished from prior art food products
like those derivable from the starch of D3. Said claims are therefore considered to
lack clarity and novelty as a consequence thereof (Article 84 and 54 EPC),

The Applicant is asked to delete claims directed to a food product.
