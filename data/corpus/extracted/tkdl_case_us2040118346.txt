9. Claim 9 is rejected under 35 U.S.C. 101 because the claimed invention is not
directed to patent eligible subject matter. Based upon an analysis with respect to the
claim as a whole, claim(s) 9 do not recite something significantly different than a judicial
exception. The rationale for this determination is explained below:

Claim 9 is directed to a method of using isolated macelignan. As evidenced by

Hwang et al, macelignan is not naturally occurring because it occurs naturally in

Myristica ragrans (ie. nutmeg). There is no structural difference between the
‘macelignan ofthe instant claims and the macelignan of nutmeg because removal or
‘golaion” of the macelignan from nutmeg did not change its stucture. Further, claim 9
recites only a general method step of administering the compound, which is a

Conventional and routine method step that amounts nothing more to a field of use.
